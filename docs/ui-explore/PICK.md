# Direction pick

Screenshots: three directions rendered on the real replay renders at 1280 and 375 (kept out of the repo because they
show a real face). Scored against the hierarchy contract in `docs/ui-plan.md`.

| Direction | Timeline first viewport at 1280 | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A Mapping sheet | Face fills the viewport; the five-tile strip falls below the fold, so the scrub is not visible with the face | Runner-up. Keep its giant year numeral |
| B Pigment ledger | Face, year, drift chip, pigment and strip all in one view; the modifier sits beside the face | **Pick for the timeline** |
| C Consent form | No scrub; reads as paperwork, which is right for the end of the flow | **Pick for the consult card** |

Pick: B for the timeline and choose screens, with A's heavy year numeral, and C's ruled-form layout for the consult
card. Reason: the signature moment needs the face, the year and the drift chip in one frame, and B is the only one
that holds all three at 1280 and stacks cleanly at 375.

Finding: the hand-placed brow string-line sat off the brow in every render. An overlay that does not match the face
is a false claim. The string-line stays on the capture screen as a framing guide only; it is never drawn on a render.
